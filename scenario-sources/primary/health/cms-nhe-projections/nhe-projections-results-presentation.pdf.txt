Major Findings
• Health spending is projected to rise faster than the economy each year from
2025 through 2034
• Continued high growth in utilization (among most services) and spending on
retail prescription drugs are the main drivers in 2025-26
• Significant legislative provisions are anticipated to affect health spending and
insurance coverage trends through 2028
• The insured share of the population is projected to decline through 2028
• The federal government share of health costs is projected to increase through
2034, mainly from Medicare
