What You Need to Know

NEGOTIATION PROGRAM

The Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program allows Medicare to directly negotiate
with drug companies to lower the prices for certain high-cost prescription drugs.

CENTERS FOR MEDICARE & MEDICAID SERVICES

The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) selected 15 drugs paid for under
Medicare Part D prescription coverage for the second cycle of negotiations and has
reached agreement for all 15 drugs on new, negotiated lower prices drugs that will go into effect in 2027. These are
some of Medicare’s most costly and most used medications. They treat a variety of conditions, such as cancer,
diabetes, and asthma.
The Medicare Drug Price Negotiation Program will improve access to life-saving drugs for people with Medicare and is
expected to save Medicare billions of dollars, strengthening Medicare now and for generations to come.
