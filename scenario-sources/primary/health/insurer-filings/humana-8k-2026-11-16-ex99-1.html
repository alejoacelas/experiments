Humana Announces Pricing Terms of Offers to Purchase for Cash Certain of its Outstanding Debt Securities
Louisville, KY - November 16, 2026 - Humana Inc. (NYSE: HUM) (“Humana”, “we”, or “our”) today announced the Reference Yield and Total Consideration (as set forth in the table below) to be paid in connection with its previously announced cash tender offers (collectively, the “Offers”) to purchase the outstanding notes described below, in each case upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Offer to Purchase dated November 9, 2026 (the “Offer to Purchase”).
The Notes offered to be purchased in the Offers, in the order of acceptance priority, are the 2.150% Senior Notes due 2032; 3.125% Senior Notes due 2029; 3.700% Senior Notes due 2029; 4.875% Senior Notes due 2030; 1.350% Senior Notes due 2027; 3.950% Senior Notes due 2027; 5.375% Senior Notes due 2031; and 5.750% Senior Notes due 2028 (collectively, the “Notes” and each a “Series of Notes”) for the consideration described below, up to an aggregate purchase price, excluding the Accrued Coupon Payment (as defined below), of $1,500,000,000 (the “Tender Cap”) subject to proration and the application of the Acceptance Priority Levels set forth in the table below and as further set forth in the Offer to Purchase and the terms and conditions, including, among others, an $800,000,000 cap on the aggregate principal amount to be accepted for purchase of the 5.375% Senior Notes due 2031 (the “2031 Notes”) pursuant to the Offers (the “Series Cap”) and the Acceptance Priority Procedures set forth in the Offer to Purchase. The 2031 Notes may be subject to proration both due to the Acceptance Priority Procedures and the Series Cap such that Humana will only accept for purchase an aggregate principal amount of the 2031 Notes up to the Series Cap. Subject to applicable law, Humana may, but is under no obligation to, eliminate, increase or decrease the Tender Cap and/or the Series Cap. In the event proration is required with respect to a Series of Notes, Humana will multiply the principal amount of each valid tender of such Series of Notes by the applicable proration rate and round the resulting amount down to the nearest integral multiple of the Authorized Denomination, in order to determine the principal amount of such tender that will be accepted pursuant to the applicable Offer. The excess principal amount of Notes not accepted from the tendering Holders will be promptly returned to such Holders, and if this excess principal amount of Notes is less than $1,000, Humana may either accept or reject all such tendering Holders’ validly tendered Notes in its sole discretion. Additionally, Humana may increase the amount of Notes accepted for payment in any Offer by no more than 2% of the outstanding Notes of the applicable Series, as further described in the Acceptance Priority Procedures set forth in the Offer to Purchase, without amending or extending the Offer. The Offer to Purchase and any related documents are referred to herein collectively as the “Tender Offer Documents”. Capitalized terms used but not defined in this press release have the meanings given to them in the Offer to Purchase.

The “Total Consideration” for each Series of Notes payable per each $1,000 principal amount of such Series of Notes validly tendered for purchase is based on the applicable Fixed Spread for such Series of Notes, plus the Reference Yield based on the bid-side price of the applicable Reference Security as quoted on the applicable Bloomberg Reference Page as of 3:00 p.m., New York City time, today, November 16, 2026 (the “Price Determination Date”). In addition to the applicable Total Consideration, Holders whose Notes are accepted for purchase pursuant to an Offer will receive accrued and unpaid interest on those Notes from the last interest payment date with respect to those Notes to, but excluding, the Settlement Date (the “Accrued Interest,” and the payment thereof, the “Accrued Coupon Payment”).
Tenders of Notes of a Series may be validly withdrawn at any time at or prior to 5:00 p.m., New York City time, today, November 16, 2026 (the “Withdrawal Deadline”), but, except as provided in the Offer to Purchase or required by applicable law, may not be validly withdrawn thereafter. The Offers will expire at 5:00 p.m., New York City time, today, November 16, 2026 (the “Expiration Date”). The “Settlement Date” will be the second business day after the Expiration Date and is expected to be November 18, 2026.
The complete terms and conditions of the Offers are set forth in the Tender Offer Documents. Holders are urged to read the Tender Offer Documents carefully. Each Offer is subject to the satisfaction or waiver of certain conditions. If any condition to the Offers is not satisfied or waived, Humana is not obligated to accept for payment, purchase or pay for, and may delay the acceptance for payment of, any tendered Notes, in each case subject to applicable law, and may terminate or alter any or all of the Offers.
