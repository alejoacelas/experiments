E A R N I N G S R E L E A S E

ELEVANCE HEALTH REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS;
RAISES FULL-YEAR GUIDANCE

•3Q 2026 operating revenue of $50.4 billion, up 0.7% from 3Q 2025
•3Q 2026 diluted EPS1 of $6.27 and adjusted diluted EPS2 of $6.99; results reflect improved operating performance and a net non-operating benefit of $1.85 per share
•FY 2026 diluted EPS1 guidance raised to at least $22.00; adjusted diluted EPS2 guidance raised to at least $29.00 on continued operating momentum
•FY 2026 operating cash flow guidance raised to at least $6.5 billion
•Investing in capabilities that improve the member experience, strengthen our competitive advantage, and support long-term growth

Indianapolis, IN - October 21, 2026 - Elevance Health, Inc. (NYSE: ELV) reported third quarter 2026 results ahead of expectations.

"Elevance Health's third quarter results reflect stronger execution across our diversified portfolio and underscore the earnings power of the business. We are raising our 2026 earnings outlook and remain confident in delivering at least 12% growth in adjusted diluted earnings per share in 2027 from our higher earnings baseline of at least $26.753. By bringing together the capabilities of our Health Benefits business and Carelon, we are improving the healthcare experience, strengthening our competitive advantage, and creating long-term value for our shareholders."

Gail K. Boudreaux
President and Chief Executive Officer

Operating revenue was $50.4 billion in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $0.4 billion compared to the prior year quarter. This was driven by higher premium yields, increased CarelonRx product revenue, and growth in service fees, partially offset by lower Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, and Employer Group risk membership.
The benefit expense ratio of 90.6 percent improved 70 basis points, driven by better performance in Medicare Advantage and Individual ACA compared to the prior year.
Days in Claims Payable stood at 44.9 days as of September 30, 2026, an increase of 3.5 days year over year.
The operating expense ratio was 11.4 percent. The adjusted operating expense ratio was 11.3 percent, an increase of 90 basis points from the prior year. The increase principally reflected targeted, one-time investments in capabilities that support our long-term performance, along with spending to advance business growth and quality initiatives.
Cash Flow & Balance Sheet
Operating cash flow of $1.3 billion in the quarter increased $0.1 billion relative to the prior year, primarily reflecting favorable working capital dynamics. As of September 30, 2026, cash and investments at the parent company totaled approximately $2.4 billion.
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company repurchased 0.8 million shares of its common stock for $330 million, at a weighted average price of $398.75 per share, and paid a quarterly dividend of $1.72 per share, representing a distribution of cash totaling $373 million. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had approximately $5.0 billion of Board approved share repurchase authorization remaining.

About Elevance Health
Elevance Health is a lifetime, trusted health partner whose purpose is to improve the health of humanity. The company supports consumers, families, and communities across the entire healthcare journey – connecting them to the care, support, and resources they need to lead better lives. Elevance Health’s companies serve approximately 103 million consumers through a diverse portfolio of industry-leading medical, pharmacy, behavioral, clinical, home health, and complex care solutions.
