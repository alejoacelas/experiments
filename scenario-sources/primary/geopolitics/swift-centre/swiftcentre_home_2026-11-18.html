Client work

How organisations have used us

Three engagements that show the shapes this work takes — from outsourcing a forecast entirely, to building the capability inside your own team.

Forecast outsourcing

Google DeepMind

Use Swift’s expert network of forecasters for an outside view of your risks.

We worked with Google DeepMind, who had developed a set of AI capability evaluations and reached out to Swift Centre to forecast how future AI systems might perform on those evaluations, and whether those capabilities would elevate AI’s status as a global issue. Swift Centre tasked a team of top forecasters with relevant AI experience to explore these topics directly with the client, who had instant access to our forecast platform to read analysis, ask questions and make clarifications in real time. The final forecasts that we produced were integrated into their paper. This paper was published and shared well beyond the client firm, helping firm up their position of influence in this area.

Joint forecast teaming

Metrea Aerospace

Work closely with Swift’s team to build a suite of predictions for your business decision making.

Metrea Aerospace had a highly skilled internal team focused on local and global risks to their business in a volatile region. Swift’s forecasters joined with the client-side experts to create a temporary ‘superteam’, marrying the internal expertise from the client with broader macro forecasting from Swift. We hosted an online and in-person workshop made up of Swift forecasters and client experts, facilitated by Swift, to explore and estimate risks to the client’s business. Swift’s final risk report enabled the client to make plans for this region with greater confidence, as well as benefit from transfer of forecasting skills to the firm.

Workshops and facilitation

Open Nuclear Network

Bring experts and best forecasting practices together.

Our client, Open Nuclear Network, convened a conference of highly technical experts in an important geopolitical risk area. Swift’s team hosted and acted as facilitators to help this group develop a series of forecasts to inform the client’s focus over the period following the workshop. We took the group through some principles of forecasting, calibration exercises and forecast software on-boarding before starting the elicitation process, covering much more ground than is possible in traditional pen-and-paper methods. The final estimates informed risk response over the next several years.
