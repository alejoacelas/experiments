MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT FOR THE
2026 WGA THEATRICAL AND TELEVISION BASIC AGREEMENT
This Memorandum of Agreement ("this Agreement") is entered into between the Writers Guild
of America, West, Inc. and the Writers Guild of America, East, Inc. (collectively "WGA" or "the
Guild"), on the one hand, and the Alliance of Motion Picture and Television Producers, Inc.
("AMPTP"), on behalf of the Companies listed in the attached Exhibit "A," and American
Broadcasting Companies, Inc., an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of ABC, Inc., CBS
Broadcasting Inc., and NBC LLC ("the Networks"), on the other hand.
The provisions of the 2023 WGA – AMPTP Theatrical and Television Basic Agreement ("the
2023 WGA – AMPTP MBA") (including all sideletters), as amended by this Memorandum of
Agreement and subject to conforming changes, and as such provisions may be amended pursuant
to Article 62, "Contract Adjustment Committee" thereof, shall together constitute and be known
as the "2026 Writers Guild of America – Alliance of Motion Picture and Television Producers
Theatrical and Television Basic Agreement" or "the 2026 WGA – AMPTP MBA." The
provisions of the 2023 WGA – Network Theatrical and Television Basic Agreement ("the 2023
WGA – Network MBA") (including all sideletters), as amended by this Memorandum of
Agreement and subject to conforming changes, and as such provisions may be amended pursuant
to Article 62, "Contract Adjustment Committee" thereof, shall together constitute and be known
as the "2026 Writers Guild of America – Network Theatrical and Television Basic Agreement"
or the "2026 WGA – Network MBA." The terms of this Agreement shall prevail over any
inconsistent terms of the 2023 WGA – AMPTP MBA or the 2023 WGA – Network MBA.
Contract language in this Agreement is indicated by quotation marks or by a notation that the
provision is written in contract language.
The terms and conditions set forth in this Memorandum of Agreement shall be effective as of
May 2, 2026 or the first Sunday following the business day on which the AMPTP receives
receipt of notice of ratification by the memberships of WGA West, Inc. and WGA East, Inc.,
whichever is later, unless a contrary date is specified, in which case the modification shall be
effective as of the date specified

EXHIBIT B
Amendments to the Health Fund Plan of Benefits
The Board of Trustees of the Health Fund shall adopt amendments to the Health
Fund recommended by the bargaining parties as provided below, and such amendments
shall provide that only the bargaining parties, not the trustees, have the authority to undo
these changes:
a. Effective January 1, 2027, the annual in-network and out-of-network deductibles will
increase to $500 for individuals and $1,500 for families. Effective on January 1 of 2028 and
each year of the Agreement thereafter, such revised deductibles will increase by an additional 3%
(with the resulting amount rounded up to the next highest $5 increment). By way of example of
the first annual 3% increase, and for the avoidance of doubt, effective January 1, 2028, the
individual and family deductibles will increase to $515 ($500 + ($500 x .03)) and $1,545 ($1,500
+ ($1,500 x .03)), respectively.
b. Effective January 1, 2027, the Coinsurance Out-Of-Pocket (OOP) Limit will increase
from $1,000 per person to $2,500 per person. Effective on January 1 of 2028 and each year of
the Agreement thereafter, such revised Coinsurance OOP Limits will increase by an additional
3% (with the resulting amount rounded up to the next highest $5 increment).
c. Effective January 1, 2027, the prescription drug coverage offered to Medicare-eligible
retirees will be changed to an employer group waiver plan (EGWP).
d. Effective January 1, 2027, The Industry Health Network benefit/arrangement will be
eliminated.
e. Effective January 1, 2027, monthly premiums for the PPO benefit increase from $0 for
participants and $50 for dependent coverage to $75 for participant only, $150 for participant +1
and $200 for family. Effective on January 1 of 2028 and each year of the Agreement thereafter,
such revised premiums will increase by an additional 3% (with the resulting amount rounded up
to the next highest $1 increment).
f. Effective January 1, 2027, in-network co-insurance will change from 85% to 80% for
the Anthem PPO.
g. Effective January 1, 2027, the out-of-network reimbursement for behavioral health
shall change from 80th percentile of Fair Health to 150% of Medicare (same as current nonbehavioral health).
h. Effective January 1, 2027, a Centivo in-network only plan will be implemented as an
alternative benefit option for actives and pre-65 retirees. The design will be as follows:
o Narrow network: Centivo Partners Network (including UCLA Health)
o No deductible in network
