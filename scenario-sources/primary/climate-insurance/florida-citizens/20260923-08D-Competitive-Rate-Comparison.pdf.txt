Competitive Rate Comparison Services
Contract Summary

Competitive rate comparison services provide a solution for analyzing and
reporting on Citizens’ rate competitiveness in the Florida property
insurance market. These services perform accurate rate comparisons by
evaluating the rates of Citizens’ policy data portfolio and comparing them
to the rates of Florida property insurers.
