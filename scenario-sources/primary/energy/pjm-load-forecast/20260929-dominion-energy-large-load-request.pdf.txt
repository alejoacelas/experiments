Quantile Process to Model Demand
Forecast leverages 13 years of observed utilization rates and all available contracts
1. Statistically model billed demand for all connected load and all future load based on
contracts using the relationship of demand to contract capacity (utilization rate) and
energization date
2. Model Components
o High billing demand forecast based on current energization date
o Low billing demand forecast based on historical energization trends
o Official billing demand forecast to align with the average of the High and Low
o MWH sales based on forecasted billing demand and historical load factor
o Upper and lower quantiles on Official forecast based on utilization rate thresholds
3. Calculate coincident demand using historical ratio (Coincident / Billing demand)
