Vistra Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Earnings Release Highlights
•GAAP third quarter 2026 Net Income of $1,140 million, including an unrealized gain from hedges expected to settle in future years of $559 million.
•Achieved more than 50% growth in Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA1 to $2,378 million for the quarter compared to third quarter 2025.
•Raised the midpoint and narrowed the range for 2026 Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA1 to $7.1 billion to $7.5 billion and Ongoing Operations Adjusted FCFbG1 to $4.3 billion to $4.7 billion.
•Initiated 2027 Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA1 and Ongoing Operations Adjusted FCFbG1 guidance ranges of $7.7 billion to $8.5 billion and $4.3 billion to $5.1 billion, respectively.
•Board authorized an additional $1.5 billion of share repurchases, which is expected to be utilized by year-end 2028.
•In October 2026, closed the acquisition of Cogentrix Energy, consisting of approximately 5,500 MW of natural gas-fueled generation capacity.
•Commenced operations of our 405 MW Pulaski Solar Facility in Illinois, under a power purchase agreement with Microsoft, and our 100 MW Deer Creek Solar & Energy Storage Facility in California.
•Announced a 20-year 207 MW power purchase agreement with New Era Energy and Digital at our Odessa combined cycle gas plant in Texas.
IRVING, Texas — Nov. 6, 2026 — Vistra Corp. (NYSE: VST) today reported its third quarter 2026 financial results and other highlights.

"I am proud of the results the Vistra team delivered this quarter, " said Jim Burke, president and CEO of Vistra. "Through one of the hottest calendar third quarters on record, our generation team kept the fleet running safely and reliably while our retail team delivered for our customers. At the same time, our people moved forward a significant amount of commercial and development work. I want to thank each one of our team members for their hard work and dedication - it's what truly sets Vistra apart."

"That effort shows in our results, with Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA up more than 50% year over year. This summer brought record weather across our markets, including the new all-time peak demand records in July in both ERCOT and PJM. Through that period, our fleet achieved commercial availability of nearly 95%. Our disciplined approach to spring maintenance paid off when our customers and communities needed reliable power most. With the Cogentrix acquisition now closed, we've added approximately 5,500 MW of new natural gas-fueled generation to our portfolio, and we continue to be well positioned for the growing demand ahead."

For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, Vistra reported Net Income of $1,140 million and Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA1 of $2,378 million. Net Income for the third quarter 2026 increased $488 million compared to the third quarter 2025, driven primarily by higher EBITDA and an increase in unrealized mark-to-market gains of $375 million on derivative positions, partly offset by higher depreciation and amortization expense driven primarily by three months' contribution from the plants acquired from Lotus, the partial impairment of the Moss Landing 350 facility, and higher taxes. Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter 2026 increased by $797 million compared to the third quarter 2025, driven primarily by higher realized energy and capacity prices, three months' contribution from the plants acquired from Lotus, and the return of Martin Lake Unit 1 to operational status.

Share Repurchase Program

As of Nov. 2, 2026:
•Vistra executed ~$6.7 billion in share repurchases since November 2021.
•Vistra had ~339 million shares outstanding, representing a ~30% reduction of the amount of the shares outstanding on Nov. 2, 2021.
•Vistra’s Board of Directors authorized an additional $1.5 billion of share repurchases. Accordingly, ~$2.5 billion of share repurchase authorization remains available, which we expect to complete no later than year-end 2028.

Liquidity
As of September 30, 2026, Vistra had total available liquidity of approximately $8,923 million, including cash and cash equivalents of $2,743 million, $4,714 million of availability under its corporate revolving credit facility, and $1,466 million of availability under its commodity-linked revolving credit facility. Available capacity under the commodity-linked revolving credit facility reflects the borrowing base of $1,466 million and excludes $284 million of commitments under the facility that were not available to be drawn as of September 30, 2026. Total available liquidity at such date excluded $1,375 million of capacity under Vistra's accounts receivable financing facilities.

In October and November 2026, Vistra redeemed all $1 billion of its Series A Preferred Stock, paid approximately $2.2 billion in cash as part of its acquisition of Cogentrix Energy, borrowed the full capacity under its accounts receivable financing facilities, and repaid approximately $1.7 billion of debt assumed in the acquisition of Cogentrix Energy, reducing available liquidity to approximately $5.4 billion on a pro forma basis.

About Vistra

Vistra (NYSE: VST) is a leading, Fortune 500 integrated retail electricity and power generation company based in Irving, Texas, that provides essential resources to customers, businesses, and communities from California to Maine. Vistra is a leader in transforming the energy landscape, with an unyielding focus on reliability, affordability, and sustainability. About Non-GAAP Financial Measures and Items Affecting Comparability

"Adjusted EBITDA" (EBITDA as adjusted for unrealized gains or losses from hedging activities, transition and merger expenses, non-cash compensation expenses, nuclear decommissioning trust income, asset retirement obligation expenses, and certain other items described from time to time in Vistra’s earnings releases), "Adjusted Free Cash Flow before Growth" (or "Adjusted FCFbG") (cash from operating activities excluding changes in margin deposits and working capital and adjusted for maintenance capital expenditures, other net investment activities, and other items described from time to time in Vistra’s earnings releases), "Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA" (adjusted EBITDA less adjusted EBITDA from Asset Closure segment), and "Ongoing Operations Adjusted Free Cash Flow before Growth" or "Ongoing Operations Adjusted FCFbG" (adjusted free cash flow before growth less cash flow from operating activities from Asset Closure segment before growth) are "non-GAAP financial measures." A non-GAAP financial measure is a numerical measure of financial performance that excludes or includes amounts so as to be different than the most directly comparable measure calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP in Vistra’s consolidated statements of operations, comprehensive income, changes in stockholders’ equity and cash flows. Non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for the most directly comparable GAAP measures. Vistra’s non-GAAP financial measures may be different from non-GAAP financial measures used by other companies.

Vistra uses Adjusted EBITDA as a measure of performance and believes that analysis of its business by external users is enhanced by visibility to both Net Income prepared in accordance with GAAP and Adjusted EBITDA. Vistra uses Adjusted Free Cash Flow before Growth as a measure of liquidity and performance, and believes that analysis of capital available to allocate for debt service, growth, and return of capital to stockholders is supported by disclosure of both cash provided by (used in) operating activities prepared in accordance with GAAP as well as Adjusted Free Cash Flow before Growth. Vistra uses Ongoing Operations Adjusted EBITDA as a measure of performance and Ongoing Operations Adjusted Free Cash Flow before Growth as a measure of liquidity and performance, and Vistra’s management and board of directors have found it informative to view the Asset Closure segment as separate and distinct from Vistra’s ongoing operations. The schedules attached to this earnings release reconcile the non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable financial measures calculated and presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP.
