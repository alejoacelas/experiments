Meta (META) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Revenue surged 26% as AI tools and Muse adoption drive engagement gains.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Oct 21, 2026 at 8:38PM EST

+Fool.comon

Image source: The Motley Fool.

DATE

Wednesday, Oct. 21, 2026

CALL PARTICIPANTS

  • Director of Investor Relations - Rob Bevegni
  • Chief Executive Officer - Mark Elliot Zuckerberg
  • Chief Financial Officer - Susan J. Li

TAKEAWAYS

  • Revenue -- $64.6 billion, growing 26% year over year on both a reported and constant currency basis.
  • Family Daily Active People (DAP) -- 3.64 billion on average for September 2026, representing a 3% increase year over year.
  • Family of Apps Ad Revenue -- $63.1 billion, increasing 26% year over year driven by ad performance gains and user engagement.
  • Ad Impressions -- 10% increase year over year, supported by ad load optimizations and healthy growth across all regions.
  • Average Price Per Ad -- 14% increase year over year, reflecting improved macroeconomic conditions and system performance gains.
  • GAAP Diluted EPS -- $3.83, including a $9.89 billion charge related to the multistate attorneys general agreement.
  • Adjusted Diluted EPS -- $7.40 excluding the impact of the $9.89 billion litigation charge recognized in the third quarter.
  • Operating Margin -- 20%, which would have been 35% if the legal settlement charge were excluded.
  • Reality Labs Revenue -- $443 million, decreasing 6% due to lower Quest headset sales, partially offset by growth in AI glasses revenue.
  • Capital Expenditures -- $34.9 billion, driven by investments in servers, data centers, and network infrastructure.
  • Free Cash Flow -- negative $2.1 billion, reflecting a significant increase in capital investments for AI infrastructure.
  • Muse Adoption -- 10 million users in the United States and Canada within six weeks of launch, with 25% using the service daily.
  • Threads Engagement -- time spent per user increased by over 20% year over year as the platform overtook X in monthly active users.
  • Partnership Ads Revenue -- $15 billion annual run rate, representing growth of over 97% year over year.
  • Meta 1 Subscriptions -- 15 million subscriptions and trials reached since the global expansion of the unified service.
  • Generative AI Ad Tools -- nearly 10 million advertisers used at least one generative AI creative tool during the quarter.
  • Video Generation Adoption -- 25% quarter-over-quarter growth among advertisers using video generation features.
  • FY 2026 Revenue Guidance -- $73 billion to $76 billion for the fourth quarter, assuming a 1% foreign currency headwind.
  • FY 2026 Total Expense Guidance -- $175 billion to $179 billion, raised to incorporate the $9.9 billion legal charge.
  • FY 2026 Capital Expenditure Guidance -- $135 billion to $145 billion, narrowed from the previously stated range.
  • Reality Labs Loss Guidance -- $18 billion to $19 billion for 2026, with losses expected to narrow to approximately $15 billion in 2027.
  • Headcount -- 66,775 employees as of Sept. 30, 2026, a 15% decrease year over year.
  • Content Processing Efficiency -- 80% reduction in per-unit costs achieved by migrating understanding models to the first-party Muse model family.
  • Ad Conversion Gains -- JEM model for ads ranking drove a 9.6% increase in app ad conversions during the quarter.

RISKS

  • Li warned that "we continue to see scrutiny on youth related issues in several markets, and have a number of youth related trials scheduled in the coming months in the United States," regarding potential negative legal and regulatory impacts.

SUMMARY

Meta Platforms, Inc. (META -0.18%) reported a total revenue increase driven by ad performance gains and sustained user engagement across its Family of Apps. Management announced the establishment of a new nonvoting Class C capital stock and a corresponding 2-for-1 stock split expected to begin trading in late Jan. 2027. The company is focusing its operational strategy on the Muse personal superintelligence platform, which achieved significant early adoption in North America. Meta is realigning Reality Labs investments to prioritize AI integration while projecting a decrease in segment losses for the upcoming fiscal year.

  • Zuckerberg stated that the company plans to focus on pushing the frontier of intelligence and distilling those models into smaller, more efficient product models optimized for personal agents.
  • Management expects to improve the cost efficiency of Muse by more than tenfold in the next year to enable segment profitability once revenue reaches scale.
  • The company completed the global launch of a generative retrieval architecture for Facebook, which increased Reels watch time by 1% by surfacing relevant new posts before they accumulate engagement.
  • Li noted that nearly 45% of recommended Reels are now less than one day old, an increase of more than 11 percentage points from the previous quarter.
  • The company announced the personal agent protocol, an open standard developed with industry partners like Stripe and Shopify to define secure interactions between consumer and business agents.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • Family of Apps (FoA): The business segment including Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and WhatsApp.
  • Reality Labs (RL): The division focused on augmented and virtual reality hardware, software, and content.
  • Muse: Meta's personal AI agent platform designed to deliver personal superintelligence.
  • Threads: A social media application for text-based updates and public conversations.
  • DAP (Daily Active People): A metric representing the number of unique users who visited at least one Meta app in a day.
  • Meta 1: A unified subscription service for consumers, creators, and businesses across Meta's platforms.
  • Muse Wonder: Meta's first frontier AI model designed for advanced reasoning and intelligence.
  • JEM: An AI model architecture used for ranking advertisements.
  • Andromeda: Meta's internal retrieval system used to scale ad delivery and complexity efficiently.
