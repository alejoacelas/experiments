Alphabet (GOOGL) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Cloud revenue surged 103% on AI demand; Gemini hit 1 billion users.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Oct 29, 2026 at 1:24AM EST

+Fool.comon

Image source: The Motley Fool.

DATE

Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026

CALL PARTICIPANTS

  • Head of Investor Relations - James Friedland
  • Chief Executive Officer - Sundar Pichai
  • Chief Business Officer - Philipp Schindler
  • Chief Executive Officer, Google Cloud - Thomas Kurian
  • Chief Financial Officer - Anat Ashkenazi

TAKEAWAYS

  • Revenue -- $127.6 billion, representing 25% year-over-year growth in both reported and constant currency.
  • Google Cloud Revenue -- $30.7 billion, increasing 103% driven by demand for AI infrastructure, AI solutions, and sales of TPU systems.
  • Google Search & Other Revenue -- $63.3 billion, reflecting 12% growth led by strong performance in retail and finance.
  • Non-GAAP EPS -- $2.12, down from $2.87 in the prior-year period due primarily to unrealized losses in the investment portfolio.
  • Google Cloud Operating Margin -- 36.2%, expanding from 23.7% in the previous year as operating income more than tripled to $11.1 billion.
  • Capital Expenditures -- $51.4 billion, with approximately 65% of technical infrastructure spend focused on servers and networking equipment.
  • Free Cash Flow -- negative $9.1 billion, impacted by high technical infrastructure investments and a $5.2 billion cash payment for a European Commission fine.
  • YouTube Advertising Revenue -- $11.2 billion, growing 9% year over year with notable strength in living room watch time.
  • Subscriptions, Platforms, and Devices Revenue -- $14.3 billion, an 11% increase driven by subscriber growth in YouTube Music and Premium.
  • AI Token Processing -- 32 billion tokens per minute, representing 40% growth from the prior quarter and a fourfold increase from the previous year.
  • Gemini App Users -- 1 billion users, making it the fastest-growing product in the company's history.
  • YouTube Subscribers -- 150 million globally for Music and Premium services, including users in trial periods.
  • Google Cloud Backlog -- $561 billion, of which the company expects to recognize approximately 55% as revenue over the next 24 months.
  • Search Query Growth -- Management reported the highest year-over-year query growth rate in five years, attributed to AI-driven usage increases.
  • TPU Performance -- The 8t generation offers 2.7-fold better training performance, while the 8i provides 80% better inference price performance over prior models.
  • Waymo Operational Scale -- 300 million fully autonomous miles driven to date, with service recently expanded to San Antonio, San Diego, and Tampa.
  • Other Income and Expense -- negative $8.2 billion, primarily reflecting an unrealized loss from a decline in the value of the company's investment in SpaceX.
  • Ad Creative AI Adoption -- 1.5 billion campaign assets were generated using Gemini models in the third quarter, a sevenfold increase year over year.
  • Inventory Balance -- $13.3 billion, up from $2.4 billion at year-end 2025 due to the buildup for TPU system sales.
  • Total Headcount -- 204,757 employees, an increase from 190,167 at the end of the prior-year period.
  • Visual Search Usage -- 50 billion visual searches per month, reflecting a shift in how users interact with Search services.

RISKS

  • Ashkenazi stated, "we would expect a slightly larger FX headwind to our consolidated revenues in Q4 relative to the prior quarter," impacting Search and YouTube advertising.
  • Ashkenazi warned that "margin headwinds in the near term" are expected in the Cloud segment as the company begins utilizing third-party compute capacity to meet demand.
  • Ashkenazi noted that the company "will continue to lap the search performance acceleration that began in the third quarter last year, which will pressure revenue growth in Q4."

SUMMARY

Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL +0.79%) reported a 25% revenue increase for the third quarter, reaching a half-trillion dollar annual run rate. Management stated that growth was driven by continued momentum in Search and a 103% revenue increase in Google Cloud, which benefited from high demand for AI infrastructure and TPU system sales. The company introduced the Gemini 4 series, including the Carbon and Argon models, while scaling AI-driven features like AI Mode and AI Overviews. While operating income rose 30% to $40.6 billion, net income was adversely affected by an unrealized investment loss in SpaceX and a substantial payment related to a European Commission legal ruling.

  • CEO Pichai reported that internal software teams using the Argon model shipped 35% more code with 30% fewer errors during the quarter.
  • The company expanded its infrastructure capacity through new partnerships with Oracle and Crux AI to deliver TPU systems to external data centers.
  • Management noted that queries in AI Mode are on average threefold longer than traditional searches, with 1 in 5 involving voice, images, or documents.
  • Google Cloud now operates 18 distinct product lines that each generate more than $1 billion in annual revenue.
  • Philipp Schindler reported that living room viewership accounted for more than one third of total YouTube advertising revenues in the quarter.
  • The company launched the Universal Cart feature to support agentic commerce, facilitating direct checkout for brands including Nike and Chewy across Google platforms.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • TAC (Traffic Acquisition Costs): Payments Google makes to partners like Apple and website owners to direct search traffic to its platforms.
  • TPU (Tensor Processing Unit): Custom-designed AI hardware developed by Google to accelerate machine learning workloads.
  • GCP (Google Cloud Platform): Google's suite of public cloud services, including compute, storage, and data analytics.
  • AI Mode: A specialized search experience designed for conversational, multimodal, and complex agentic queries.
  • OI&E (Other Income and Expense): Non-operating line items including interest income, interest expense, and gains or losses on equity investments.
  • Agentic Commerce: AI-driven shopping experiences where automated agents perform tasks like research, comparison, and transactions for users.
