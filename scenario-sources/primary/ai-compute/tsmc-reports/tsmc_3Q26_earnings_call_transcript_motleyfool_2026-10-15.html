TSMC (TSM) Q3 2026 Earnings Call Transcript

Revenue surged 41% year-over-year as AI chip demand pushed HPC to 68% of sales.

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Oct 15, 2026 at 11:38AM EST

+Fool.comon

Image source: The Motley Fool.

DATE

Thursday, Oct. 15, 2026

CALL PARTICIPANTS

  • Director of Investor Relations - Jeff Su
  • Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer - Wendell Huang
  • Chairman and Chief Executive Officer - C.C. Wei

TAKEAWAYS

  • Net Revenue (U.S. Dollars) -- $46.72 billion, representing 16.2% sequential growth and 41.2% growth year over year.
  • Net Revenue (NT Dollars) -- TWD 1,494.24 billion, an increase of 50.9% year over year and 17.6% sequentially.
  • Net Income -- TWD 763.06 billion, up 68.7% compared to the prior year.
  • Diluted EPS -- TWD 29.43, equivalent to $4.60 per ADR unit.
  • Gross Margin -- 66.4%, representing a sequential decrease of 1.3 percentage points primarily due to margin dilution from the 2 nm technology ramp-up.
  • Operating Margin -- 58.7%, exceeding the guidance range of 56% to 58%.
  • Net Profit Margin -- 51.1%, compared to 45.7% in the same quarter last year.
  • 2 nm Revenue Share -- 8% of total wafer revenue, following the commencement of high-volume manufacturing in the fourth quarter of 2025.
  • 3 nm Revenue Share -- 33% of total wafer revenue, reflecting continued adoption of leading-edge technologies.
  • Advanced Technology Revenue -- 79% of wafer revenue, comprising nodes 7 nm and below.
  • HPC Platform Revenue -- 68% of total revenue, increasing 22% sequentially driven by structural demand for AI accelerators.
  • Smartphone Platform Revenue -- 21% of total revenue, representing a 10% sequential increase.
  • Automotive Platform Revenue -- 4% of total revenue, showing 23% sequential growth.
  • IoT Platform Revenue -- 4% of total revenue, representing 5% sequential growth.
  • DCE Platform Revenue -- 1% of total revenue, declining 12% quarter over quarter.
  • Q4 Revenue Guidance -- $52.3 billion to $54.3 billion, representing 14% sequential growth at the midpoint.
  • Full Year 2026 Revenue Guidance -- 44% growth in U.S. dollar terms.
  • 2026 Capital Budget -- $60 billion to $64 billion, reiterated to support robust multiyear demand from AI and HPC applications.
  • Q4 Gross Margin Guidance -- 65% to 67%, reflecting continued dilution from the steep 2 nm ramp-up and overseas fab operations.
  • Q4 Operating Margin Guidance -- 56.5% to 58.5%.
  • Cash and Marketable Securities -- TWD 4 trillion, or approximately $126 billion, at the end of the quarter.
  • Inventory Days -- 82 days, a decrease from 87 days in the second quarter due to increased wafer shipments.
  • N2 Capacity Expansion -- Management expects annual capacity for the N2 family of technologies to more than double in 2027.
  • A14 Technology Timeline -- Risk production is scheduled for 2027, with volume production expected to begin in 2028.
  • Arizona Fab 2 Status -- Volume production is scheduled to begin in the third quarter of 2027, slightly ahead of the previous schedule.

RISKS

  • Wei stated, "consumer and price-sensitive end market segment are being challenged due to the impact of rising component prices and macroeconomic uncertainties," indicating a prudent approach to 2027 business planning.
  • Huang warned that gross margin dilution from overseas fab ramp-ups is expected to "widen to 3%-4% in the next few years" as more facilities begin operations in the U.S.

SUMMARY

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company Limited (TSM -3.69%) reported third-quarter results driven by demand for advanced process technologies, specifically within the high-performance computing and artificial intelligence segments. Management increased its full-year revenue growth outlook to 44% in U.S. dollar terms while maintaining capital expenditure targets for 2026. The company is currently executing a multiyear expansion strategy involving approximately 20 fabs in various stages of progress worldwide, including a shift toward larger-format photomasks for High NA EUV by 2030. Leadership noted that while certain consumer-facing segments face macroeconomic headwinds, the underlying demand for AI accelerators, networking chips, and advanced packaging remains above the company's current production capacity.

  • CEO Wei stated, "AI-related demand is being supported by strong underlying demand for AI accelerators," noting the transition from chatbots to agentic AI assistants.
  • Management indicated that the company is adding two new 3 nm fabs in Tainan with volume production starts scheduled for the first half of 2027 and the first half of 2028.
  • Wei reported that N2 entered high-volume manufacturing in the fourth quarter of 2025 and is ramping successfully in Hsinchu and Kaohsiung.
  • The company is evaluating further expansion in the U.S., with teams assessing opportunities in Texas and potential cooperation at a Terafab site.
  • CFO Huang stated that capital expenditures from 2026 to 2028 are expected to be "even more significantly higher than the past three years" to support structural customer growth.
  • The company reported that advanced packaging capacity for 5x reticle size is in production, with development underway for 9x and 14x reticle sizes.
  • Wei noted that risk production for A14 technology is on track for 2027, and management expects A14 to be a larger node than N2.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • HPC: High-performance computing, referring to the use of supercomputers and parallel processing techniques for complex modeling and data analysis.
  • 2 nm / 3 nm / 5 nm / 7 nm: Advanced semiconductor manufacturing process nodes where lower numbers represent smaller, more dense, and more power-efficient transistor technology.
  • ADR: American Depositary Receipt, a certificate representing shares of a foreign company that are traded on a U.S. stock exchange.
  • CoWoS: Chip on Wafer on Substrate, a specialized 2.5D packaging technology that integrates multiple chips into a single package.
  • High NA EUV: High Numerical Aperture Extreme Ultraviolet lithography, an advanced chipmaking tool used to print extremely fine circuit patterns.
  • Agentic AI: Artificial intelligence systems designed to act autonomously as assistants to perform specific tasks or workflows.
  • Reticle Size: The maximum exposure area of a lithography machine in a single step, influencing the size of the chips that can be manufactured.
  • TIFRS: Taiwan Financial Reporting Standards.
  • DCE: Digital Consumer Electronics.
