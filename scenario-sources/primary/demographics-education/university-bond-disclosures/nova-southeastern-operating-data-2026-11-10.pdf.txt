Enrollment and Credit Hour Production Statistics
The following table shows the University's enrollment statistics, based upon actual registrations for the Fall Term of 2022 through 2025 along with the Fall Term of 2026 which is expected
to be reported to the Federal Government in April 2027.
Beginning Fall Term 2023, undergraduate students pursuing a bachelor's degree in the field of nursing but not yet satisfying the prerequisites for the Bachelor of Science in Nursing degree
program (hereafter referred to as Nursing Pathway students) are reported in the Ron and Kathy Assaf College of Nursing, which is part of the Health Professions Division. Previously,
Nursing Pathway students were reported in the Halmos College of Arts and Sciences. Based on available data, historical headcount for these two colleges for the Fall Term 2022 has
been recast to conform to the Fall Terms 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026 classification.
Effective July 1, 2025, the Dr. Pallavi Patel College of Health Care Sciences became two schools, the Dr. Pallavi Patel School of Health Sciences and the Dr. Pallavi Patel School of
Rehabilitative Sciences. The Dr. Pallavi Patel School of Health Sciences was integrated into the Dr. Kiran C. Patel College of Allopathic Medicine and the Dr. Pallavi Patel School of
Rehabilitative Sciences was integrated into the Dr. Kiran C. Patel College of Osteopathic Medicine. In August 2025, the Engineering department moved from the College of Computing
and Engineering to the Halmos College of Arts and Sciences, and the College of Computing and Engineering was renamed The College of Computing, AI, and Cybersecurity. As a result of
these 2025 realignments, historical information for the Fall Terms 2022, 2023, and 2024 have been recast into the new colleges housing the programs to be consistent with the Fall
Terms 2025 and 2026 presentation in order to provide for comparability.
Beginning fiscal 2025, the University no longer separately reports a Health Professions Division (HPD). To be consistent with the presentation in the relevant official statements, the
table below continues to reflect data for the prior HPD colleges under HPD.

To the extent an application is made by a prospective student to more than one program and/or campus location, each application, and respective acceptance if applicable, is included
within the totals reported for Applications and Acceptances. Prior to Fall Term 2023, a new student enrolled at the University was counted as a matriculate for each program and/or
campus location to which the student was accepted, consistent with the manner of counting applications and acceptances. Effective Fall Term 2023, the methodology for calculating
matriculates was revised to count a student as a matriculate only for the program and/or campus location in which the student ultimately enrolled. The total reported for Matriculates
beginning Fall Term 2023 reflects the revised methodology. Applying the revised methodology to Fall Term 2022, Matriculates would have been 2,721.
