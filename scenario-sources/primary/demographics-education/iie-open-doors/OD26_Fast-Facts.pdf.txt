O T H E R F O R M S O F E D U C AT I O N A B R O A D
In addition to the 313,824 U.S. students who received academic credit for
study abroad in 2024/25, 349 institutions reported that 32,992 students
participated in non-credit experiential activities, such as work, internships,
volunteering, and research abroad. Furthermore, 108 U.S. institutions reported
that 20,423 students participated in online global learning opportunities,
including remote internships, collaborative project-based learning, and videoconference dialogues.
