Projections of Education Statistics

The Projections of Education Statistics provides projections for key education statistics, including enrollment, graduates, teachers, and expenditures in elementary and secondary public and private schools, as well as enrollment and degrees conferred at degree-granting postsecondary institutions. Each edition provides revisions of prior estimates and includes forecasts roughly ten years beyond the latest available historical data at the time the forecasts were produced.
