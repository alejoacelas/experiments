Method and limits

  • Each area was researched separately, then every score was re-read against the anchors and adjusted so that a 4 means the same thing in every area.
  • Top picks are the highest combined scores in an area, subject to covering different trends and mechanisms. Near-duplicates were held back to the longer list.
  • A document counts as downloaded in full only if the complete official file is on disk and opens. Text captures, summaries and single volumes of a set count as partial. All files, {{GB}} GB and {{PAGES}} pages of them, are in the repository under scenario-sources/primary/, indexed in manifest.json.
  • Every quote was checked by script against the text of the downloaded file. The check ignores spacing and punctuation but requires the exact word sequence. Quotes that failed were removed, not edited.
  • Accuracy conditions describe incentives and structure. They are not a measured hit rate, and plans made under strong incentives still slip: capacity plans run late and utilities have a record of over-forecasting load.
  • The source set leans to the United States because that is where filings are most detailed and most public.
